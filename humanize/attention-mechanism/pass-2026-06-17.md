## Before / after — lede (unchanged, recorded for reference)

> When people say a model 'attends' to a word, it sounds like a metaphor. It
> isn't. Attention is a precise, repeatable step: every token in a sentence
> quietly reads every other token, scores how much each one matters, and keeps a
> blend of what it found. Do that in parallel, many times over, and you have a
> transformer.

rhythm, concrete payoff. Kept as-is.
