| Myth | Reality | Claim IDs |
|------|---------|-----------|
| ANC “eats” sound or creates a vacuum of silence | It adds a second pressure wave that interferes destructively at the ear | C-03 |
| ANC works equally well on all frequencies | Active paths are strongest at low frequencies; highs rely more on passive isolation | C-20, C-33 |
| Turning ANC on replaces the need for a seal | Fit/leakage still matter for both passive and the control plant | C-30–C-32, US9747887 |
| One mic placement does everything | Feedforward and feedback sense different places and solve different problems | C-10–C-12 |
| Perfect cancellation is possible in consumer headphones | Phase/amplitude error, latency, and nonstationary noise leave residuals | C-02, C-14 |
