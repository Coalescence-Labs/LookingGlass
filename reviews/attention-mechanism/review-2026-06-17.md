### A11y nits (non-blocking)

- **N1** Touch targets: HeadSelector (`px-3 py-2`) and row buttons (`py-1.5`)
  are ~32–40px tall, just under the 44px ideal on small phones. Functional but
  could be bumped.
- **N2** The 100 informational cells are `aria-label`'d `<div>`s; a screen
  reader navigating element-by-element could find them verbose. The primary SR
  path (row buttons + live caption) is clean, so acceptable for a data viz.
